LLM SELL SCORECARD // WITH vs WITHOUT THE LLM (BOTH PAPER ACCOUNTS)
How to read this scorecard

Every logged sell-layer decision is scored only once its PREDECLARED forward window has completed — no mid-window peeking. The delta is the realized LLM branch minus the engine's own branch, in percentage points of the decision fill, net of the declared one-way costs and cash yield. CONFIRM/HOLD rows (and fail-safes, which fall back to the engine's rule) are no-divergence: delta 0, counted separately. The reference bars are the pre-measured blanket rules from the research (A: blanket defer +2.93pp/event; B: blanket retention −1.60pp/event; C: the M5 reconciliation) shown as context — "the LLM vs the blanket rules". Small samples are reported as-is: NO p-values, NO significance claims. The fail-safe rate is itself a verdict on the reviewer.

Savings accounting rules
Real money — never touched by "Reset All Sims" or any backfill. Interest accrues daily at rate/365 on the cash balance and pays out monthly, like Trade Republic. "Record transaction" books each event with its own type: a Deposit moves fresh money in (a contribution); a Withdrawal moves fresh money out (an external flow that lowers net contributed capital — never a loss; only a true-up reconciles drift); Sparplan buys and one-off Buys deduct from the tracked cash and add an equal-allocation picks mirror entry (each amount split equally across the Daily-Core picks available at the time, at latest cached prices — a picks comparison, not Daily-Core strategy performance); a Saveback books TR's 1% bonus (new money, cash untouched); a Sell books the disposal and its proceeds arrive when you record the Settlement; "True-up" only reconciles drift. A missing price defers a Sparplan buy to the next pass.

PAPER ACCOUNT MAINTENANCE // REPLAYS & RESETS

Replays and resets are destructive to recorded paper history. A backfill replaces the account's state with a synthetic replay of the strategy. A reset clears the account's current book and starts a new epoch for the Daily Sim, Monthly and Daily-Core paper accounts (their closed-epoch rows stay in the database for audit but no longer feed the charts). Exception — Daily Sim reset also deletes the DCA benchmark curve and book without preserving an epoch (the benchmark is a derived comparison artifact, not a paper account with an epoch ledger). Market data is kept. Savings (real EUR) is never touched.

Backfills & resets are disabled on PROD — the live paper history is append-only. Switch the database to DEV in Settings to replay or reset.
